Straightforward Process

How a Search Works

What happens after you send us an open role.

01

You send the role

Tell us the role title, site location, shift, pay band, and the non-negotiable licenses, certifications, or equipment experience required.

02

A person calls you

We call within one business day to confirm the jobsite details, interview process, and put our fee agreement in writing before starting.

03

We screen first

We check state license numbers and issuing registries, confirm hands-on tool or control-system experience, and verify shift, commute, and pay fit.

04

You pay if they start

Reviewing candidate profiles and interviewing costs nothing. Our direct-hire fee applies only when a candidate you hire starts on Day 1.

We keep notes on what you accept or pass so the next introduction is closer. Candidates can correct or delete their information at any time — see our Privacy Policy.

Contingency Terms

How Our Fees Work

15%–25% of Base Pay

The exact percentage depends on the role and is locked in a signed written agreement before our search starts.

2,080-Hour Base Only

Overtime, bonuses, and per diem are excluded. Hourly roles are calculated on base hourly rate × 2,080 hours.

$0 Due Until Start

Nothing is owed upfront. Invoice date, payment terms, and replacement guarantee terms are specified in your signed agreement.
