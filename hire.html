Vendor & Hiring Manager Overview

What We Screen & How We Onboard

Before we submit a candidate to your team, we verify primary credentials and confirm role logistics. Below is our screening checklist by discipline and what is included in our vendor onboarding packet.

01 // LICENSED TRADES & POWER

What "Screened" Means

  • Issuing state authority & license number recorded
  • License expiration date & active status checked
  • Voltage classes & test equipment (Doble, Megger, SEL)
  • Confirmation of base pay, overtime, per diem & shift
02 // ENGINEERING & CONTROLS

What "Screened" Means

  • Degree, FE/PE license status, or commissioning levels
  • PLC/SCADA software environments written from scratch vs. edited
  • On-site commissioning vs. remote support willingness
  • Base salary band & relocation/travel alignment
03 // FIELD SUPERVISION

What "Screened" Means

  • Direct crew size & subcontractor oversight history
  • Project types (data center, substation, industrial plant)
  • OSHA 30 / CHST / NFPA 70E training dates
  • Notice period & mobilization date
Vendor Packet (Sent on Request)

Approved-Vendor Onboarding

When you submit a role or email recursiverecruiting@gmail.com, we provide our standard vendor packet for your Finance and HR teams before any candidate introductions occur:

  • 1. Written Contingency Fee Agreement (specifying exact %, 2,080-hr base basis, and exclusions)
  • 2. Invoice trigger (Day 1 start), payment terms, and replacement guarantee window in writing
  • 3. Candidate permission & data-handling policy (Right-to-Represent per role)
  • 4. Completed W-9 and direct billing contact details
